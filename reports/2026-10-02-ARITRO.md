## 6. Known bug — dashboard priority cards (not fixed)

The dashboard widget claims the lead in the database, then keeps showing that same lead. It does not rotate to the next unclaimed hot lead.

Observed: the Hot Leads tile said 10. The Priority AI Alerts box showed 3 cards, already marked Claimed. CRM still had unclaimed New cards with a Claim Lead button. Those are two different controls.

`dashboard/page.tsx:127-129`:

```tsx
const priorityLeads = leads
  .filter(l => l.lead_temperature?.toLowerCase() === 'hot')
  .slice(0, 3)
```

That keeps the first 3 hot leads. It does not drop `conversion_status === 'claimed'`. The "10 high-intent buyers" sentence uses `hotCount` (`page.tsx:105`), which is every hot lead, claimed or not.

Claim itself works. `PriorityAlertCard.tsx:17-18` calls `acknowledgeLeadAlert`, then sets local state to claimed (`PriorityAlertCard.tsx:9`, `18`). It does not remove the card or ask for the next hot lead. `main.py:1085-1086` marks the notification acknowledged. `main.py:1088-1091` loads the lead by `id` and `client_id`, then sets `conversion_status = "claimed"`. A refresh still returns those 3, because they are still hot. The widget then paints them as Claimed from `lead.conversion_status`.

CRM Claim is a different rule. `KanbanBoard.tsx:168` shows the button only on the New column, or if `is_negotiating`, and only when the lead is not already claimed. Unclaimed New cards can still be claimed there. Contacted cards cannot, unless they are negotiating.

Fix, later, not in this pass: filter claimed leads out before `slice(0, 3)`, and refetch or drop the card after a successful claim. Do not change the CRM button for this.
